PRIVACY NOTICE

Designed for student privacy.

This static prototype stores profile, questionnaire and feedback data in your browser's local storage. It does not provide production-grade security or real authentication.

Before launch

  • Use a secure backend and server-side sessions/OAuth or a vetted auth provider.
  • Encrypt data in transit and at rest; minimize what you collect.
  • Use role-based access controls so admins only see authorized records.
  • For minors, implement appropriate consent, age handling and guardian requirements for the jurisdictions you serve.
  • Publish retention/deletion rules and let students request deletion or correction.
  • Do not train an AI model on student data by default; document exactly how AI providers receive and retain prompts.
  • Keep analytics aggregated and avoid exposing identifiable student information.
Your Path uses cookies/storage.

We use local browser storage in this prototype to remember login state, questionnaire progress and cookie preferences. Production analytics/marketing cookies should be opt-in where required.
